### 🛡️ Safety & Insurance

**Is IC Clean insured?**
Yes, fully insured. A certificate of insurance is available upon request — just ask.

**Can I trust your team in my home?**
We take this seriously. IC Clean is a small, personal team with a consistent standard of care — whoever shows up at your door. We treat every home with the same respect and attention we'd want for our own.

**What if something gets damaged during the clean?**
In the unlikely event that something is damaged, we are fully insured and will handle it professionally and transparently. Your peace of mind matters to us.
