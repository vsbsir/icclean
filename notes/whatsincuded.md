### 🧹 What's Included / Add-Ons

**What's included in a regular cleaning?**
Our regular service covers all the essentials — dusting, vacuuming, mopping, kitchen and bathroom cleaning, light switches, baseboards, and more. Check our Services page for the full breakdown.

**Can I request extra tasks not listed in the standard service?**
Absolutely. Our service lists are a starting point, not a ceiling. If you have specific areas or tasks you'd like us to focus on, just let us know when booking and we'll make it happen.

**Do you offer add-ons?**
Yes! Depending on the service, add-ons include fridge and oven cleaning, inside cabinets, interior windows, and more. Just ask and we'll tailor the clean to your needs.
