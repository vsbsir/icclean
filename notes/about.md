# 👋 ABOUT US

## Headline
**We're Not a Corporation. We're Just Really Good at Cleaning.**

## Body Text
IC Clean started the way most honest businesses do — by spotting a problem and deciding to fix it.

Garen noticed that finding a cleaning service you could actually trust was harder than it should be. Too many companies hid behind fancy marketing, inflated promises, and impersonal service. What people really wanted was simple: a reliable team that shows up, does the job properly, and treats their home with respect. So that's exactly what IC Clean became.

What started as a small operation quickly grew — not through gimmicks, but through word of mouth. Happy clients, honest work, and a reputation that speaks for itself.

Today, IC Clean is still a small, personal team — and that's intentional. We're not trying to be the biggest cleaning company in the city. We're trying to be the best one for you. Whoever you speak to, whoever shows up at your door — the standard is always the same. Consistent, thorough, and genuinely cared for.

When we come into your home, we treat it like our own. That means paying attention to the details others might skip, using HEPA-filter vacuums that capture fine dust and allergens (not just push them around), and never calling a job done until you've seen it and are happy with it.

No cleaning degrees. No corporate script. Just a small team that genuinely cares about the work they do — and the homes they do it in.
