We're Not a Corporation. We're Just Really Good at Cleaning.

IC Clean started the way most honest businesses do — by spotting a problem and deciding to fix it.

Garen noticed that finding a cleaning service you could actually trust was harder than it should be. Too many companies hid behind fancy marketing, inflated promises, and impersonal service. What people really wanted was simple: a reliable team that shows up, does the job properly, and treats their home with respect. So that's exactly what IC Clean became.

Today, IC Clean is still a small, personal team — and that's intentional. We're not trying to be the biggest cleaning company in the city. We're trying to be the best one for you. When we come into your home, we treat it like our own. That means paying attention to the details others might skip, using HEPA-filter vacuums that capture fine dust and allergens, and never calling a job done until you've seen it and are happy with it.

No cleaning degrees. No corporate script. Just a small team that genuinely cares about the work they do — and the homes they do it in.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a cleaning?
Simple — just reach out through our contact form or give us a call. We'll get back to you promptly to confirm the details, walk you through the service, and lock in a date and time that works for you.

How far in advance do I need to book?
We recommend reaching out as early as possible to secure your preferred date, especially for deep cleaning, move-in/out, and post-renovation services which require a larger team.

Do I need to be home during the cleaning?
For the first clean, yes — and honestly, we think it's a good idea regardless. It lets you meet the team, align on expectations, and do a walk-through together at the end. After that first visit, we're happy to work out whatever arrangement suits you.

How is pricing determined?
Regular cleaning starts at $35/hr per person for the initial visit. Every home is different, and until we've seen yours we don't want to guess — the hourly rate keeps things fair for everyone. After the first clean, we provide a fixed price based on your home's size, condition, and preferred frequency.

Deep cleaning, move-in/out, and post-renovation services are $40/hr per person. We're always happy to chat through the details before you commit to anything.

Is there a minimum number of hours?
No. We're here for as long as the job needs, and not a minute more than necessary.

Are there hidden fees?
Never. What we quote is what you pay.

What's included in a regular cleaning?
All the essentials — dusting, vacuuming, mopping, kitchen and bathroom cleaning, light switches, baseboards, and more. See our services section for the full breakdown.

Can I request extra tasks?
Absolutely. Our service lists are a starting point, not a ceiling. Just let us know when booking and we'll make it happen.

Do you offer add-ons?
Yes — fridge and oven cleaning, inside cabinets, interior windows, and more. Just ask and we'll tailor the clean to your needs.

Is IC Clean insured?
Yes, fully insured. A certificate of insurance is available upon request — just ask.

Can I trust your team in my home?
We take this seriously. IC Clean is a small, personal team with a consistent standard of care — whoever shows up at your door. We treat every home with the same respect and attention we'd want for our own.

What if something gets damaged?
In the unlikely event that something is damaged, we are fully insured and will handle it professionally and transparently.

Do you bring your own equipment?
Yes, always. We arrive fully equipped — you don't need to provide a thing.

What vacuum do you use?
HEPA-filter vacuums, which capture fine dust and allergens rather than just redistributing them. It makes a real difference — especially for allergy sufferers or homes with pets.

Do you use eco-friendly products?
We're happy to discuss product preferences when you book. Just let us know if you have any sensitivities and we'll do our best to accommodate.

Are there height restrictions?
Our team works with a 4-step ladder, reaching approximately 4–5 feet. Very high ceilings or tall fixtures may be out of reach — just mention it when booking and we'll discuss what's possible.

Do you clean chandeliers?
Simple chandeliers, yes. Elaborate or high-value chandeliers with crystals or delicate components — we respectfully decline. We'd rather be honest upfront than risk something that can't be replaced.

What if I need to cancel or reschedule?
Life happens — just reach out as soon as you know and we'll work with you to find a new time. We only ask for reasonable notice so we can adjust the team's schedule.

What if I'm not satisfied?
We do a walk-through with you at the end of every clean for exactly this reason. If something isn't right, we'd rather know on the spot and fix it than have you unhappy. Your satisfaction is the whole point.
