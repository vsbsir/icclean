**Your regular clean, done right.**

We know your home is your space — and we treat it that way. Our recurring cleaning service covers all the essentials so you can come home to fresh, every time.

**What's included:**

*General Areas (Living Rooms, Bedrooms, Hallways):*
Dusting and wiping all surfaces, vacuuming and mopping all floor types, wiping and disinfecting light switches, straightening and organizing common areas, making beds (linen changes available on request), cleaning glass partitions, dusting baseboards, and removing cobwebs from corners and ceilings.

*Kitchen:*
Cleaning appliance surfaces, cabinets, and scrubbing the sink, vacuuming and mopping the floor, and polishing stainless steel appliances.

*Bathrooms:*
Cleaning fixtures, showers, and drawers, cleaning mirrors, vacuuming and mopping the floor, and emptying trash bins with liner replacement as needed.

*Laundry Room:*
Cleaning surfaces of the sink, washer, and dryer, vacuuming and mopping the floor.

*Staircases:*
Vacuuming and mopping the stairs.

*Mirrors & Glass:*
Cleaning all mirrors and partition glass throughout the home.

**Flexible scheduling:**
Book once, or set up a recurring schedule — bi-weekly, every 3 weeks, monthly, or as needed. Whatever works for you.

**Your safety matters:**
We are fully insured (certificate available upon request), bring our own equipment and cleaning solutions, and use HEPA-filter vacuums to capture fine dust and allergens — keeping your home not just clean, but genuinely healthy.

**Our quality promise:**
Every clean ends with a walk-through inspection together with you to make sure everything meets your expectations. No minimum hours required — we're here for as long as the job needs.

*Every visit is treated like the first — because your home deserves nothing less.*

**For when "regular clean" just won't cut it.**

**What's included:**

*Living Areas:*
Dusting all furniture, shelves, electronics, and décor, vacuuming upholstery and floors, mopping hard surfaces, cleaning interior windows (glass, ledges, and screens), wiping doors, frames, knobs, light switches, and baseboards, cleaning staircases and railings, and tidying common areas.

*Kitchen:*
Cleaning outside, inside, and behind the refrigerator and oven, wiping countertops, backsplash, cabinets (inside optional), and sink, polishing stainless steel appliances, and vacuuming and mopping floors.

*Bathrooms:*
Scrubbing and disinfecting toilets, sinks, tubs, and showers, cleaning tiles and shower floors, cleaning interior windows (glass, ledges, and screens), vacuuming and mopping floors, emptying trash bins with liner replacement, and cleaning inside drawers on request.

*Bedrooms:*
Dusting all surfaces, décor, and furniture, vacuuming carpets and mopping hard floors, cleaning interior windows (glass, ledges, and screens), wiping doors, frames, knobs, light switches, and baseboards, and general tidying and organization.

*Laundry Room:*
Cleaning the utility sink, wiping washer/dryer exteriors (interior disinfection available on request), vacuuming and mopping floors, and general tidying.

*Entryway:*
Wiping the entrance door, frame, and hardware, vacuuming and mopping floors.

*Garage & Utility Areas (upon request):*
Sweeping garage floor and cleaning mechanical/utility areas.

**Your safety matters:**
We are fully insured (certificate available upon request), bring our own equipment and cleaning solutions, and use HEPA-filter vacuums to capture fine dust and allergens.

**Our quality promise:**
Every clean ends with a walk-through inspection together with you to make sure everything meets your expectations. No minimum hours required — we stay until the job is truly done.

*We don't cut corners. We clean them.*

### 📦 Move In / Out Cleaning
**One less thing to stress about on moving day.**

Moving is already a lot. Between boxes, logistics, and a hundred things on your list, cleaning shouldn't be one of them. We'll take care of the space so you can focus on everything else.

**What's included:**

*Living Areas:*
Dusting all surfaces, walls, and ceiling corners, vacuuming and mopping floors, cleaning doors, frames, handles, and light switches, deep cleaning baseboards and vents, cleaning interior windows (glass, ledges, and screens), and cleaning staircases and railings.

*Kitchen:*
Deep cleaning all surfaces, countertops, and backsplashes, cleaning appliances inside, outside, and behind (including fridge and oven), wiping down all cabinets inside and out, and vacuuming and mopping floors.

*Bathrooms:*
Deep cleaning and sanitizing toilets, sinks, bathtubs, and showers, cleaning tiles, mirrors, and fixtures, wiping down cabinets inside and out, vacuuming and mopping floors, and trash removal.

*Bedrooms:*
Dusting all surfaces, walls, and ceiling corners, cleaning interior windows (glass, ledges, and screens), vacuuming and mopping floors, and wiping down light switches, baseboards, and door frames.

*Laundry Room:*
Cleaning washer and dryer exteriors, sink cleaning, and vacuuming and mopping floors.

**Your safety matters:**
We are fully insured (certificate available upon request), bring our own equipment and cleaning solutions, and use HEPA-filter vacuums to capture fine dust and allergens.

**Our quality promise:**

### 🔨 Post-Renovation Cleaning
**Your renovation is done. Now let's make it actually livable.**

Renovations are exciting — the dust, debris, and construction residue left behind? Not so much. Post-renovation cleaning is a whole different level of clean, and it's not something a regular mop and bucket can handle. We come in after the contractors leave and make sure your newly refreshed space is truly ready to enjoy.

**What's included:**

*Dust & Surface Cleaning:*
Thorough dusting of all surfaces including walls, light fixtures, and exposed beams, and wiping down all horizontal and vertical surfaces to remove construction dust and residue.

*Floor Cleaning:*
Sweeping, vacuuming, and mopping all floor types — carpets, hardwood, tile, and linoleum — with detailed edge cleaning to remove dust and debris from corners and baseboards.

*Windows & Glass:*
Interior window cleaning to remove dust and streaks, wiping down ledges and frames, and removal of any construction residue or adhesive on glass surfaces.

*Doors & Frames:*
Cleaning doors, frames, and handles to remove dust and fingerprints, and spot-cleaning door knobs and fixtures.

*Cabinets & Drawers:*
Wiping down all interior and exterior cabinet and drawer surfaces, including removing any protective adhesive or construction dust.

*Appliances:*
Cleaning the exterior of kitchen and laundry appliances, removing any remaining packaging or residue, cleaning inside, outside, and behind the fridge and oven, and wiping the interior of new appliances if needed.

*Bathrooms:*
Thorough cleaning of all fixtures, tiles, and floors to remove dust and grime, and sanitizing toilets, sinks, and showers.

*Light Switches & Baseboards:*
Wiping and disinfecting light switches, and dusting and cleaning baseboards to remove construction dust and debris.

*Debris & Trash Removal:*
Removal of small construction debris and waste left from renovation, and emptying trash bins with liner replacement as needed.

*Garage & Basement (upon request):*
Sweeping the garage floor and cleaning basement technical rooms.

*Final Touches:*
Wiping down stair railings, handrails, and bannisters, spot-cleaning walls for smudges or dust marks, and cleaning any additional furniture or shelving as required.

**Your safety matters:**
We are fully insured (certificate available upon request), bring our own equipment and cleaning solutions, and use HEPA-filter vacuums to capture fine dust and allergens — essential after any renovation project.

**Our quality promise:**
Every clean ends with a walk-through inspection together with you to make sure everything meets your expectations. No minimum hours required — we stay until every trace of the renovation is gone.

*We handle the aftermath — so all you see is the finished result.*
