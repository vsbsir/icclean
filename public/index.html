👋 About Us

We're Not a Corporation. We're Just Really Good at Cleaning.

IC Clean started the way most honest businesses do — by spotting a problem and deciding to fix it. Garen noticed that finding a cleaning service you could actually trust was harder than it should be. No cleaning degrees. No corporate script. Just a small team that genuinely cares about the work they do — and the homes they do it in.

Equipment & Products

🤝 Do you bring your own equipment and supplies?

Yes, always. We arrive fully equipped with everything needed to get the job done — you don't need to provide a thing.

🏠 What kind of vacuum do you use?

We use HEPA-filter vacuums, which capture fine dust and allergens rather than just redistributing them. It's one of those details that makes a real difference — especially for allergy sufferers or homes with pets.

🌬️ Do you use eco-friendly products?

We're happy to discuss product preferences when you book. Just let us know if you have any sensitivities or specific requests and we'll do our best to accommodate.

✅ Are there any height restrictions?

Yes, and we'd rather be upfront about it. Our team works with a 4-step ladder, which reaches approximately 4–5 feet. This means we may have limitations when it comes to very high ceilings, tall light fixtures, or hard-to-reach areas in spaces like entrance halls and staircases. If your home has high ceilings or specific elevated areas you'd like cleaned, just let us know when booking and we'll discuss what's possible.

🛡️ Do you clean chandeliers?

It depends. Simple, straightforward chandeliers we're happy to clean — but for elaborate or high-value chandeliers with crystals or delicate components, we respectfully decline. It's not that we couldn't try — it's that we wouldn't feel right taking the risk with something that can't be replaced easily. We'd rather be honest about that upfront than take a chance with your valuables. If you have a chandelier you'd like us to take a look at, just mention it when booking and we'll have an open conversation about what we can and can't do.

Pricing & Payments

How is pricing determined?

For our regular cleaning service, we charge $35/hour per person for the initial clean. We do it this way for a simple reason — every home is different, and until we've seen yours we don't want to guess. A fixed price upfront risks overcharging you for something straightforward, or undercharging ourselves for something that needs more attention. The hourly rate on the first visit keeps things fair for everyone. After that, we'll provide a fixed price for ongoing services based on your home's size, condition, and preferred frequency.

For deep cleaning, move-in/out, and post-renovation services, the rate is $40/hour per person. These services involve a larger team and a more intensive scope of work, so pricing is based on the size and condition of the space. We're always happy to chat through the details before you commit to anything.

Is there a minimum number of hours required?

Nope. We have no minimum hours requirement — we're here for as long as the job needs, and not a minute more than necessary.

Are there any hidden fees?

Never. We believe in straightforward, honest pricing. What we quote is what you pay.

👋 FAQ SECTION

Booking & Scheduling

**How do I book a cleaning?**

Simple — just reach out through our contact form or give us a call. We'll get back to you promptly to confirm the details, walk you through the service, and lock in a date and time that works for you.

**How far in advance do I need to book?**

We recommend reaching out as early as possible to secure your preferred date, especially for deep cleaning, move-in/out, and post-renovation services which require a larger team.

**Do I need to be home during the cleaning?**

For the first clean, yes — and honestly, we think it's a good idea regardless. Meeting the team that comes into your home lets you put a face to the name, ask any questions, and feel confident about who you're welcoming through your door. It also means we can do a walk-through together at the start to make sure we're aligned on your expectations, and another one at the end to make sure you're happy with the results. After that first visit, we're happy to work out whatever arrangement is most convenient for you.

**What if I need to cancel or reschedule?**

We get it — life happens. Just reach out as soon as you know and we'll work with you to find a new time. We only ask for reasonable notice so we can adjust our team's schedule accordingly.

**What if I'm not satisfied with the clean?**

We do a walk-through inspection with you at the end of every clean for exactly this reason. If something isn't right, we'd rather know on the spot and fix it than have you unhappy. Your satisfaction is the whole point.
