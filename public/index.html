House & Condo Cleaning

Regular cleaning that actually stays on top of things. We handle the full sweep — kitchens, bathrooms, floors, surfaces — using HEPA-filter vacuums that capture dust and allergens instead of just pushing them around. No rotating crew, no surprises. You get the same team each visit, and we do a walk-through at the end of every clean so nothing gets missed. $35/hr per person. No minimums. No hidden fees.

Deep Cleaning

When a regular clean isn't enough. We go further — inside appliances, behind furniture, baseboards, vents, grout lines — the areas that get skipped in routine visits but build up over time. Good for a first clean before switching to regular service, a seasonal reset, or any time the place just needs a proper going-over. $40/hr per person. No minimums. No hidden fees.

Move In / Move Out

Moving is stressful enough. We take the cleaning off your list — whether you're leaving a place and want your deposit back, or moving into somewhere that needs a fresh start before you unpack. We clean top to bottom, inside cabinets and appliances included. Walk-through at the end, every time. $40/hr per person. No minimums. No hidden fees.

  • Get a Quote

Post-Renovation Cleaning

Renovation dust gets everywhere — and it keeps settling for days. We do a thorough clean after construction or reno work wraps up: fine dust on every surface, debris in corners, residue on fixtures, all of it. HEPA-filter vacuums make a real difference here. They pick up the fine particulate that a regular vacuum recirculates back into the air. $40/hr per person. No minimums. No hidden fees.

Why IC Clean

A Real Team, Not a Corporation

We're a small, hands-on crew — the same people show up every time. No franchises, no strangers, no revolving door.

HEPA-Filter Vacuums

Our vacuums trap dust and allergens instead of recirculating them. Better for your home, better for anyone with allergies or asthma.

Walk-Through at Every Clean

Before we leave, we walk through the space with you. If something isn't right, we fix it on the spot.

Transparent Pricing

$35/hr per person for regular cleaning, $40/hr for deep, move, or reno cleans. No hidden fees, no minimum hours.

Fully Insured

We carry full liability insurance and can provide a certificate on request. You're covered.

Same Team Every Visit

You'll recognize the people cleaning your home. Consistency matters — we think it makes for a better clean.
